About the Directory

Decarbonising the UK's housing stock is widely accepted to be a major priority to meet national emissions-reduction targets. The retrofit system is highly complex, interacting with policy, construction, and public health.

Our goal is to support organisations across all sectors to connect, collaborate, and share expertise seamlessly.
